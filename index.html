How the numbers work

CutGrid splits the audio into 11.6 ms frames, measures how sharply the spectrum jumps in the bass, mid and treble bands, and finds the tempo from how those jumps repeat. A beat tracker then lays the grid, and bar starts come from where the kick and snare fall. Sections come from changes in tone and loudness bar by bar, and a drop is a bar where the level and the low end both jump.

The scores are heuristics tuned on EDM, phonk, lofi, trap, ambient techno, piano and film score. Punch is how far the spectrum leaps on each beat, Pulse is how clearly the grid can be heard, Payoff is the strongest drop plus the dynamic range, Drive is how busy the hits are, and Hook rates the best 15 second window's opening second. Edit power weights them 30, 20, 20, 15 and 15. Treat them as a fast first read and trust your ears for the final call.
